Government job biodata format — what to include

  1. Post applied for and a passport-size photo (top right).
  2. Personal details — full name, father's and mother's name, date of birth, gender, category, nationality, religion and marital status if asked.
  3. Contact — mobile, email, permanent and correspondence address.
  4. Educational qualifications — a table with examination, board/university, year of passing, percentage/CGPA and subjects.
  5. Experience — post, organisation and period, if any.
  6. Languages known, skills and hobbies.
  7. Declaration with place, date and your signature.
