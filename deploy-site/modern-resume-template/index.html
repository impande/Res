What "modern" actually means on a resume

A modern resume isn't about adding colour and icons everywhere — it's about looking current and uncluttered. That comes from a clean sans-serif font, generous spacing, a clear visual hierarchy, and maybe one subtle accent colour or a tasteful two-column layout. The content still leads; the design just makes it easy and pleasant to scan.

What makes a template look modern

  • Clean sans-serif typography with clear heading styles.
  • White space that gives each section room to breathe.
  • A subtle accent colour — one, used sparingly.
  • Strong hierarchy — your name and roles stand out at a glance.
  • Optional two-column layout for a contemporary, space-efficient feel.

Modern vs creative — don't confuse them

A modern resume is polished but conservative enough for any professional role. A creative resume leans into bold colour, graphics and personality and is meant for design or creative fields. If you're in tech, marketing, business or a startup, modern is the sweet spot — current, but safe for recruiters and ATS alike.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a resume template look modern?

Clean sans-serif typography, generous white space, a subtle accent colour, clear visual hierarchy, and sometimes a tasteful two-column layout. Modern doesn't mean flashy — it means current, uncluttered and easy to scan.

Are modern resume templates ATS-friendly?

The good ones are. A modern look comes from typography and spacing, not from graphics that confuse parsers. Our modern templates are built to stay ATS-readable while still looking contemporary.

Who should use a modern resume template?

It suits most roles in tech, marketing, design, startups and corporate positions. If you're applying somewhere very traditional (some government or legal roles), a plainer layout can be safer, but modern works almost everywhere.

What's the difference between modern and creative resumes?

A modern resume is clean and current but still conservative enough for any professional role. A creative resume leans into colour, graphics and personality, and suits design or creative fields specifically.
