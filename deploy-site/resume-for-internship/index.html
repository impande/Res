Short answer: for an internship resume with no experience, lead with education and replace work history with academic projects, coursework, volunteering and skills — each with 2–3 result-focused bullet points, all on one page.

You don't need work experience for an internship resume

Internships exist precisely because you're still learning — so recruiters don't expect a work history. What they look for is potential and initiative: relevant coursework, academic or personal projects, and a clear sense of why you want the role. Your job is to fill the space a resume normally uses for experience with the things you do have.

What to include on an internship resume

1. Contact header

Name, professional email, phone, city, and one link — LinkedIn, GitHub or a portfolio.

2. Internship objective (2 lines)

State the internship you want and what you bring. Tie it to the role: a data internship objective should mention Python or statistics; a marketing one should mention campaigns or tools.

3. Education (lead with this)

Degree, institution, expected graduation year, and relevant coursework or a strong GPA.

4. Academic projects & coursework

This is what replaces experience. Pick 2–3 projects and, for each, write what you built, the tools you used and the outcome. A class project, a hackathon, or a personal build all count.

5. Skills & certifications

Technical tools and languages, relevant soft skills, and any online courses or certifications that match the internship.

Quick tips that get interviews

  • Tailor the objective and skills to each internship — mirror the words in the listing.
  • Lead bullets with results ("used by 200+ students"), not just tasks.
  • Keep it one page and use a clean, ATS-friendly layout.
  • Add a portfolio or GitHub link — proof beats claims for technical internships.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a resume for an internship with no experience?

Lead with your education, then fill the experience gap with academic projects, coursework, college activities, volunteering and any freelance work. Give each 2–3 bullet points that describe what you did and the result. Add a short objective, your skills and certifications, and keep it to one page.

What should an internship resume include?

Contact header, a two-line objective, education (first), academic projects and coursework, skills, and certifications. Add positions of responsibility or volunteering if they show initiative.

What is a good objective for an internship resume?

State the internship you want and what you bring. Example: "Second-year Computer Science student seeking a software development internship to apply hands-on Java and web project experience."

How long should an internship resume be?

One page. As a student or fresher you rarely need more, and recruiters prefer a concise single-page resume.
