Free printable & ATS-friendly resume templates

Short answer: for online applications, pick a single-column template with standard headings and plain text — it's the safest for applicant tracking systems (ATS). For printing or handing over at a walk-in interview, a two-column or more designed template is fine, as long as the text is real text and not part of an image.

Most "free resume templates" you find online are made to look good on screen or on paper. Many large employers and job portals in India, though, first pass your resume through an ATS — software that reads the text and sorts candidates before a recruiter sees anything. A template can look beautiful and still be unreadable to that software. The good news: you don't have to choose. A well-built template can be both ATS-friendly and printable.

What makes a resume template ATS-friendly?

  • Real, selectable text — no name, skills or headings baked into images, icons or graphics.
  • Standard section headings — "Summary", "Education", "Experience", "Projects", "Skills", "Certifications". Creative labels like "My Journey" confuse parsers.
  • A logical reading order — top to bottom, so the software reads your job titles, dates and bullets in the right sequence.
  • No text boxes, tables or headers/footers for key details — some ATS skip them entirely, which can hide your phone number or email.
  • One or two readable fonts at 10–12pt for body text, with simple bullets.
  • No skill bars or star ratings — "Python ●●●○○" means nothing to an ATS. Write "Python (pandas, NumPy)" instead.

Printable and design-heavy templates: what's different?

Printable templates are built for a human reading paper — at a campus placement drive, a walk-in, or when an interviewer asks for a hard copy. They often use a coloured sidebar, a photo, icons and skill graphics. That's fine on paper, but the same features can trip up an ATS. If you love a design-heavy layout, use it for printed copies and keep a plain single-column version for online applications. With one-click template switching you can make both from the same content in seconds.
