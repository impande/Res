Frequently asked questions

What is an ATS score?

It estimates how well an applicant tracking system can read your resume and how closely it matches the job title you are targeting, out of 100.

Does “Fix it for me” add things I did not do?

No. It rewrites weak lines using only facts already in your resume. You choose which changes to apply.
