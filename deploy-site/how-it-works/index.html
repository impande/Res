Why it matters

Most companies screen resumes with applicant tracking systems (ATS) before a recruiter reads them. Every main template on Resume For You is single-column and checked by extracting the PDF text the way an ATS does, so your name, contact details and sections are read in the right order. The AI writes quantified bullet points from your notes, and the ATS score shows what to fix before you apply.

Frequently asked questions

Is Resume For You free?

Yes. You can build, edit and preview your resume free with no sign-up. Downloading the PDF has a small one-time fee; job alerts and interview practice on WhatsApp are free.

Are the templates ATS-friendly?

Yes. All 100+ main templates are single-column and were checked by extracting the PDF text the way an applicant tracking system reads it — name first, sections in order, nothing missing. Nine optional two-column designs are labelled “Less ATS-safe”.

How long does it take to make a resume?

Most people finish in 5–10 minutes. Add your details, let the AI write your bullet points, pick a template and download.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — the builder works on any phone, tablet or computer, and you can also build your resume by chat on WhatsApp.
