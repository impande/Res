Frequently asked questions

Are all templates ATS-friendly?

All 100+ main templates are single-column and checked the way an applicant tracking system reads a PDF. Nine optional two-column designs are in a separate tab labelled “Less ATS-safe”.

Will I lose my content if I switch templates?

No. Your content stays the same — only the design changes.
