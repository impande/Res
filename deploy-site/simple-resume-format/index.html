Short answer: the best simple resume format is a single-column, reverse-chronological layout with clear headings, standard fonts and no graphics — one page, easy for recruiters to scan and safe for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS).

Why a simple resume format wins

It's tempting to reach for a colourful, multi-column template — but most of them work against you. Over 90% of mid-to-large companies filter resumes through an Applicant Tracking System first, and ATS software routinely scrambles tables, text boxes and columns. A simple, single-column format is read correctly every time, and it lets a busy recruiter find your name, role and skills in seconds. Simple isn't boring — it's effective.

The simple resume layout, section by section

Keep your sections in this order on one page:

  1. Header — full name, professional email, phone, city, and one link (LinkedIn or portfolio).
  2. Summary or objective — two to three lines on who you are and the role you want.
  3. Experience (or Education first, if you're a fresher) — each entry with a title, organisation, dates and 2–3 bullet points.
  4. Skills — a short, scannable list of relevant tools and strengths.
  5. Education / Certifications — degree, institution and year; courses that support the role.
  6. Optional — achievements, languages or volunteering, only if there's space.

Formatting rules for a clean resume

  • One column, no tables or graphics — the single most important rule for ATS.
  • Standard fonts — Calibri, Arial or similar at 10.5–12pt.
  • Consistent spacing and bullets — align everything and use the same bullet style throughout.
  • Plain section headings — "Experience", "Education", "Skills".
  • One page wherever possible, and export as PDF.
  • Lead bullets with results — what you did and the outcome, not just duties.

Frequently asked questions

What is a simple resume format?

A simple resume format is a clean, single-column layout with clear section headings, standard fonts and no graphics, tables or columns. It is easy for recruiters to scan and for Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to read.

Is a simple resume better than a creative one?

For most jobs, yes. Simple resumes pass ATS reliably and let recruiters find your details fast. Creative, multi-column designs often break in ATS and can hide key information. Keep design minimal unless you're applying for a visual-design role with a separate portfolio.

What is the best simple resume format for freshers?

A reverse-chronological, single-page layout that leads with education, then projects and internships. See our resume format for freshers guide for the full section order.

Should a simple resume be PDF or Word?

Send a PDF by default — it keeps your layout identical everywhere. Only use Word (.docx) if a job post specifically asks for it.
