Short answer: a fresher resume summary is 2–3 lines at the top of your resume that combine your qualification, your 2–3 strongest skills, and the role you want — written to be scanned in seconds.

Summary or objective — which should a fresher use?

A summary highlights what you offer; an objective states what you want. As a fresher you can use either, but a short summary usually reads more confidently — especially if you have projects or an internship to point to. Whichever you pick, keep it to two or three lines and tailor it to the job.

How to write a strong fresher summary (3 steps)

  1. Start with your qualification — "Computer Science graduate", "B.Com fresher", "Mechanical engineering graduate".
  2. Add your 2–3 strongest, role-relevant skills — concrete tools or abilities, not vague words like "hardworking".
  3. End with the role you want — "seeking a [role]" ties it to the job.

Avoid empty adjectives ("dynamic team player"). Specifics — a language, a tool, a project outcome — are what make recruiters read on.

10 resume summary examples for freshers

IT / Software

  • Computer Science graduate skilled in Java, React and SQL, with hands-on web project experience, seeking a software developer role.
  • Fresher proficient in Python and data structures, with two academic ML projects, looking for an entry-level backend developer position.

Engineering (core)

  • Mechanical engineering graduate trained in AutoCAD and SolidWorks, with a final-year design project, seeking a graduate engineer trainee role.
  • Civil engineering fresher skilled in STAAD.Pro and site estimation, seeking a site engineer position.

Data / Analytics

  • Statistics graduate skilled in Python, SQL and Power BI, with a dashboard capstone project, seeking a data analyst role.

Marketing

  • Marketing graduate experienced in SEO, Meta Ads and content writing through college campaigns, seeking a digital marketing executive role.

Commerce / Finance

  • B.Com graduate skilled in Tally, Excel and GST basics, with an accounts internship, seeking an accounts executive role.
  • BBA fresher with strong financial-modelling and communication skills, seeking a business analyst trainee position.

Non-technical / general

  • Graduate with strong communication and organisational skills and a customer-service internship, seeking an operations associate role.
  • B.A. graduate fluent in English and Hindi with content-writing experience, seeking a content associate position.

Frequently asked questions

What is a resume summary for a fresher?

A resume summary (or professional summary) is a 2–3 line statement at the top of your resume that highlights your qualification, key skills and the value you offer — tailored to the role you want. For freshers it draws on education, projects and skills rather than work experience.

What is the difference between a summary and an objective?

A summary highlights what you offer (your strengths and skills); an objective states what you want (the role you're targeting). Freshers can use either — a summary works well when you have projects or internships to point to, an objective when you have less to show. Keep whichever you choose to 2–3 lines.

How do I write a resume summary with no experience?

Combine your qualification, 2–3 strongest skills, and the role you want into two or three lines. Example: "Computer Science graduate skilled in Java and web development, with hands-on project experience, seeking a software developer role." Use concrete skills, not vague adjectives.

How long should a fresher resume summary be?

Two to three lines (about 30–50 words). It should be scannable in a few seconds and sit right below your contact details.
