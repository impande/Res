How this ATS checker scores your resume

Most companies run resumes through an Applicant Tracking System before a recruiter reads them. This checker grades the same things those systems and recruiters care about, in seven areas:

  • Parseability & contact — standard headings, clean single-column text, full contact details.
  • Structure — clear Summary, Experience, Education and Skills sections with consistent dates.
  • Impact — how many bullets carry real numbers and outcomes (the heaviest weight).
  • Action verbs & clarity — strong, concise bullets with no filler.
  • Skills & keywords — role-relevant skills that also appear in your bullets.
  • Experience depth and length — 1 page early in your career, up to 2 pages after 10+ years.

Your overall score is a fixed weighted average of those areas, so the same resume gets the same score every time. Resumes over three pages, or without achievement bullets, are capped — exactly the resumes that struggle in real screening.

ATS checker FAQ

What is a good ATS score?

75–84 is good and 85+ is strong. Most resumes land between 56 and 74. Scores of 93+ are rare.

Why is my score capped?

Over-length resumes (3+ pages) and resumes without achievement bullets are capped, because both perform poorly in real screening. Trim to 1–2 pages and add quantified bullets to lift the cap.

Does this check against a specific job?

This is a resume-only readiness check. To match a specific job post, use the free builder's "Tailor to a job description".

My PDF won't read — what now?

Scanned (image-only) PDFs have no selectable text. Paste your resume text instead and you'll get the same score.
