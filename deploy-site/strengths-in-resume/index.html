Short answer: pick 3–5 strengths that match the job and prove each one in your bullets with a result, rather than just listing adjectives. Strengths are personal qualities (leadership, problem-solving); skills are teachable abilities (Python, Excel).

Strengths vs skills — know the difference

Skills are specific and teachable: Python, Tally, SEO, AutoCAD. Strengths are broader personal qualities: leadership, adaptability, problem-solving. Recruiters value both, but a list of strengths ("hardworking, dedicated, team player") means nothing on its own — they're only convincing when you show them with evidence.

How to show strengths (not just list them)

Weak: "Strengths: leadership, communication, problem-solving."
Strong: "Led a 5-person team to deliver a project two weeks early (leadership); presented findings to senior management (communication)." Let the achievement prove the strength.

Where to put your strengths

  • Professional summary — lead with your 1–2 biggest, most relevant strengths.
  • Experience bullets — demonstrate each strength with a result.
  • Skills/strengths section — optionally list a few, but keep it short and genuine.

Match your strengths to the job description, and make sure each one shows up as evidence somewhere in the resume.

Frequently asked questions

What are good strengths to put on a resume?

A mix relevant to the job: problem-solving, communication, leadership, adaptability, teamwork, attention to detail, analytical thinking, and role-specific strengths. The best ones are those you can back up with a concrete example.

What is the difference between strengths and skills?

Skills are specific, teachable abilities (Python, Excel, SEO). Strengths are broader personal qualities (leadership, problem-solving, adaptability). A strong resume shows both — and proves the strengths through achievements.

How do I show strengths on a resume instead of just listing them?

Don't only list adjectives — demonstrate them in your bullets. Instead of writing 'good leader', write 'Led a team of 5 to deliver a project two weeks early.' The result proves the strength.

Where should strengths go on a resume?

Weave your top strengths into your professional summary and your experience bullets, and optionally list a few in a skills/strengths section. Showing beats telling.

How many strengths should I include?

Focus on 3–5 that match the job, each demonstrated somewhere in your resume. Too many unproven adjectives weaken the effect.
