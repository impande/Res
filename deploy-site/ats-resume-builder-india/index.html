Why an ATS-friendly resume matters in India

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software recruiters use to collect, scan and rank resumes. When you apply through Naukri, LinkedIn or a company career page, your resume is usually parsed by an ATS first — it pulls out your name, contact details, skills, job titles and dates, then ranks you against the job description. If the software can't read your resume, a recruiter may never see it.

Common things that break ATS parsing:

  • Two-column layouts, tables and text boxes.
  • Skills or contact info inside images, icons or headers/footers.
  • Non-standard section names and decorative fonts.
  • Missing keywords from the job description.

Our builder avoids all of these by default and shows a live ATS score as you type, so you can fix issues before you apply — not after you've been filtered out.
