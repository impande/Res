How to use your questions

  1. Say each answer out loud — interviews are spoken. Aim for 60–90 seconds per answer.
  2. Fill in every [X] with your real numbers. Specific results are what make an answer memorable.
  3. Prepare the gaps listed below your questions — that's where interviewers will probe.
  4. End with a question of your own. We give you four that show genuine interest.

Interview prep FAQ

What is the STAR method?

Situation, Task, Action, Result — a structure for behavioural answers that ends on a measurable outcome.

Do I need the full job post?

It works best with the full description, but just the job title works too — you'll get questions for that role in general.

Can I practise on WhatsApp?

Yes — message us "interview" with your role and practise in chat.
