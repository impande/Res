Quick answer: fresher teacher resume format

  1. Contact details — name, phone, email, city; mention if you can relocate.
  2. Objective — the post (PRT/TGT/PGT), subject, board and your TET status.
  3. Qualifications — B.Ed or D.El.Ed first, then your subject degree, 12th and 10th.
  4. Eligibility tests — CTET or state TET: paper, subject and year.
  5. Teaching practice — school internship, written like a job, with classes and results.
  6. Tutoring, co-curricular skills and tech — home tuition, coaching, sports, Google Classroom.
  7. One or two pages, clean and error-free — schools judge your written English closely.

Your training already counts as teaching experience

Principals and school coordinators hiring freshers know you haven't held a full-time post yet. What they want to see is that you are qualified for the post, that you've actually stood in front of a class, and that you'll add something to school life beyond the syllabus. Your B.Ed or D.El.Ed school internship, micro-teaching, home tuitions and volunteering cover all three — if you write them up properly.

What schools look for in a fresher teacher

  • The right qualification for the level — D.El.Ed/JBT for primary, a subject degree plus B.Ed for middle and secondary, a postgraduate degree plus B.Ed for senior secondary.
  • TET status — CTET or your state TET, especially for CBSE schools and government posts.
  • Classroom practice — which classes you taught, how many lessons, and what changed for students.
  • Subject command and spoken English — English-medium schools test this in the interview and demo class.
  • Teaching methods — activity-based learning, lesson plans, formative assessment, use of teaching aids.
  • Tech and co-curricular value — smart boards, Google Classroom, and whether you can run the debate club, sports or music.

Step-by-step: how to write a teacher resume as a fresher

Follow these seven steps; the sample resume below uses the same order.

Step 1: Add contact details and a teaching headline

Name, phone, a professional email and your city — add “open to relocation” if true, as many schools hire from outside the city. Under your name: TGT Mathematics & Science | B.Sc. B.Ed | CTET Paper II Qualified. More ideas in resume headlines for freshers.

Step 2: Write an objective for the exact post

Schools advertise by level and subject, so match the vacancy:

  • B.Sc. B.Ed graduate seeking a TGT Mathematics post in a CBSE school; CTET Paper II qualified, with a school internship teaching Classes 6–8 through activity-based methods.
  • D.El.Ed graduate seeking a primary teacher (PRT) role; trained in play-way and phonics-based teaching, fluent in English and Hindi, CTET Paper I qualified.
  • M.A. English, B.Ed fresher seeking a PGT English post; taught Class 11 grammar and poetry during internship and coached the school debate team.
  • Science graduate seeking a tutor or coaching faculty role; two years of tutoring Class 9–10 students in Science and Maths.

More examples: career objectives for freshers.

Step 3: List qualifications with the teaching degree first

Unlike most fresher resumes, a teacher resume often lists 10th and 12th as well, because many schools check eligibility from them. Order: B.Ed/D.El.Ed → postgraduate degree (if any) → graduation → 12th → 10th, each with university or board, year and percentage. Add your pedagogy subjects (e.g. Pedagogy of Mathematics, Pedagogy of Science) — schools match these to the vacancy.

Step 4: Show CTET or state TET clearly

Give TET results their own small section right after education: CTET Paper II (Mathematics & Science) — Qualified, 2025. Add your state TET the same way. If you've appeared and are awaiting results, write “Appeared, result awaited” — honest and still useful. For pre-primary roles, mention an NTT or ECCE diploma here.

Step 5: Write your school internship like a job

This is your Teaching Experience section. Include:

  • School internship / teaching practice — school name, board, classes, subject, number of lessons, teaching aids, a measurable result.
  • Home tuitions or online tutoring — number of students, classes, results (honestly).
  • Coaching institute or part-time faculty work.
  • NSS, NGO or evening-school teaching, summer camps or workshops.

Got a recorded demo lesson? Add a link — it saves the school a step.

Step 6: Add teaching skills and co-curricular strengths

  • Teaching skills: lesson planning, classroom management, activity- and project-based learning, formative assessment, question paper setting, remedial teaching, parent communication.
  • Tech: Google Classroom, smart boards, MS PowerPoint, Canva, Google Forms quizzes, DIKSHA resources.
  • Soft skills: patience, clear explanation, adaptability, empathy.
  • Co-curricular: sports, music, dance, art, debate, quiz, chess — whatever you could actually lead.

Ideas: skills to put on a resume and hobbies in resume.

Step 7: Add training, certificates and a declaration

Short courses on SWAYAM or DIKSHA (including NISHTHA modules), workshops on inclusive education or ICT, and language certificates all fit here. Many Indian schools still expect a declaration with place, date and signature at the end — add one if the school or the vacancy notice asks for it.

Weak vs strong teacher resume bullet points

Strong bullets name the class, the method and what improved for students.

WeakDid teaching practice in a school.
StrongTaught 40 lessons of Class 7 Mathematics during my B.Ed internship using fraction kits and group activities; the class average in the unit test rose from 58% to 71%.

Fresher teacher resume mistakes to avoid

  • Not naming the post and subject. “Seeking a teaching job” gets filed away.
  • Hiding your CTET/TET at the bottom of the page.
  • Listing internship as one line instead of describing classes, lessons and results.
  • Grammar errors — schools read them as a sign of how you'll teach.
  • Inflated tuition results you can't back up when the principal asks.
  • Forgetting co-curricular skills that make you useful beyond the timetable.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a teacher resume as a fresher with no experience?

Lead with a post-specific objective, then your B.Ed or D.El.Ed and other qualifications, your CTET or state TET status, and a Teaching Experience section built from your school internship, tutoring and volunteering. Finish with teaching skills, co-curricular strengths and languages.

Should I mention CTET or TET on my resume?

Yes, in its own section right after education: the paper, subject and year, for example CTET Paper II (Mathematics and Science), Qualified, 2025. If you are awaiting results, write Appeared, result awaited.

What is the best resume format for a fresher teacher?

A single-column, one-page format in this order: contact, objective, qualifications, eligibility tests, teaching experience, skills, training and co-curricular, languages, and a declaration if the school asks for one.

What objective should a fresher teacher write?

Name the post, subject and board, then your qualification and one proof point. For example: B.Ed graduate seeking a TGT English post in a CBSE school; CTET qualified, with a school internship teaching Classes 8 and 9.

Can I count home tuitions as teaching experience?

Yes. List them under Teaching Experience with dates, classes, subjects and the number of students, and mention results only if they are true and you can explain them.

Is B.Ed or D.El.Ed needed for a primary teacher job?

D.El.Ed or JBT is the standard qualification for primary classes, and B.Ed is the usual requirement for Classes 6 and above. Rules differ by board and state and have changed in recent years, so always check the vacancy notice.

Should I add a photo to a teacher resume?

Only if the school or application form asks for one. If it does, use a recent, plain-background, professional photo.

Can I make my teacher resume in Hindi?
