Questions, answered

Is it really free for students?

Yes. Under a college plan, your students build, edit, preview and download their résumés at no cost. The college covers one simple plan; students never pay.

Do students need to install anything or create accounts?

No installs. They just open your college link and start — it works on any phone or laptop. Sign-in is optional, only if they want to save and come back.

Can we use our own branding and a standard template?

Yes — the page carries your college's name and logo, and you can set a standard template so every résumé looks consistent to recruiters.

How do we collect all the résumés?

From your dashboard you can track completion and download every student's résumé together — as individual PDFs (ZIP) or a combined booklet.

How long does setup take?
