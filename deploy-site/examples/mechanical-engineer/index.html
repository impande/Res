What to include in a mechanical engineer resume

  • Professional summary — Years, domain (automotive, HVAC, manufacturing), key CAD tools and one quantified result.
  • CAD & technical skills — SolidWorks, AutoCAD, CATIA, GD&T, FEA — the exact keywords core-industry ATS screens for.
  • Quantified projects — Cost cut, weight reduced, cycle time improved, defects lowered — numbers in every bullet.
  • Certifications — CSWA/CSWP (SolidWorks), Six Sigma, or an AutoCAD certification.
  • Education — B.E./B.Tech Mechanical with college, year and CGPA.

Frequently asked questions

What should a mechanical engineer resume include?

A summary with your domain and CAD tools, a technical skills section (SolidWorks, AutoCAD, GD&T, FEA), quantified project outcomes (cost, weight, cycle time, defects), and your B.E./B.Tech with CGPA plus any CSWA/Six Sigma certification.

How do I write a mechanical engineer resume for a fresher?

Lead with academic and internship projects: state the CAD tools used, what you designed or analysed, and the result. Add a strong skills section and CGPA, and any SolidWorks (CSWA) or NPTEL certifications.

Which software should I list?

SolidWorks, AutoCAD and CATIA for design; ANSYS for FEA; plus GD&T and any PLM/CAM tools you know. Only list what you can actually use in an interview.
