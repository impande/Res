What to include in a digital marketing resume

  • Professional summary — Your years, channels owned (SEO/paid/email) and one headline metric (traffic, ROAS, CAC).
  • Channel & tools skills — SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, SEMrush — the keywords ATS and recruiters screen for.
  • Metric-driven experience — Every bullet needs a number: traffic %, ROAS, CAC, leads, conversion rate.
  • Certifications — Google Ads, GA4, HubSpot or Meta Blueprint — list them; recruiters value them.
  • Portfolio / links — Link a portfolio, a ranked article, or a case study if you have one.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a strong digital marketing resume?

Metrics. State the channels you own and quantify results — organic traffic growth, ROAS, CAC reduction, leads and conversion rate — plus tools (Google Ads, GA4, SEO) and relevant certifications.

How do I write a digital marketing resume as a fresher?

Show projects and internships: a blog you grew, a small ad campaign, or a college fest you promoted, with real numbers. Add Google Ads and GA4 certifications (both free) and a skills section with the core channels.

Which certifications should I add?

Google Ads, Google Analytics (GA4), HubSpot Content/Inbound, and Meta Blueprint are the most recognised and several are free.
