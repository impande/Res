What to include in a graphic designer resume

  • Portfolio link — Put a Behance/Dribbble/personal-site link at the top — it is the single most important element.
  • Professional summary — Your years, design areas (branding, social, UI) and one measurable result.
  • Software & skills — Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Figma — the exact keywords design ATS screens for.
  • Quantified experience — Engagement lifts, turnaround cut, assets produced, conversion improved — add numbers where design allows.
  • Keep it ATS-safe — Use a clean, single-column template for the ATS copy; save the heavy visual work for your portfolio.

Frequently asked questions

What should a graphic designer resume include?

A portfolio link at the top, a short summary, a software/skills section (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Figma), quantified experience where possible, and your design degree. Keep the resume itself clean and ATS-friendly.

Do graphic designers need an ATS-friendly resume?

Yes. Many companies screen resumes through ATS first, so use a clean single-column layout with real text (not an image) and the right tool keywords — then let your portfolio show the creativity.

How do I make a designer resume with no experience?

Lead with your portfolio and college/freelance projects: state the brief, tools used and outcome. Add a strong software skills section and any client or competition work.
