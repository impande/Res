What to include in a hr manager resume

  • Professional summary — Years, HR functions owned (TA, HRBP, payroll) and one quantified people outcome.
  • HR skills & HRMS — Talent acquisition, payroll, HRBP, Darwinbox/SAP SuccessFactors — the ATS keywords for HR.
  • Quantified experience — Hires closed, time-to-fill, attrition, eNPS, employees managed — always numbers.
  • Compliance & tools — Labour law compliance, payroll tools, HRMS platforms you have used.
  • Education & certifications — MBA-HR or a SHRM/HRCI certification if you have one.

Frequently asked questions

What should an HR resume include?

A summary with your HR functions and one people metric, a skills section (talent acquisition, payroll, HRBP, HRMS), and quantified experience — hires, time-to-fill, attrition, eNPS — plus your MBA-HR or SHRM certification.

How do I write an HR resume for a fresher?

Highlight your MBA-HR, internships, and any live recruitment, payroll or engagement project. Add HRMS familiarity (Darwinbox, SAP) and a skills section, and quantify anything you supported.

Which HR certifications help?

SHRM-CP, HRCI (aPHR/PHR) and platform certifications (Darwinbox, SAP SuccessFactors) are the most recognised. List them clearly.
