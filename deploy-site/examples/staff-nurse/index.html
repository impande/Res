What to include in a staff nurse resume

  • Professional summary — 3–4 lines with years of experience, units worked (ICU/ER/OT), registration and certifications like BLS/ACLS.
  • Registration & certifications — State nursing council registration (RN/RM) and BLS, ACLS or specialty certifications — these are checked first.
  • Clinical skills — Patient care, critical care, medication administration, IV therapy, monitoring and EMR — the keywords hospital ATS screens for.
  • Quantified experience — Where possible add numbers: beds/patients handled, accuracy rate, shifts, juniors trained.
  • Education — B.Sc/GNM nursing with university and marks.

Frequently asked questions

What should a staff nurse resume include?

A short summary, your nursing registration (RN/RM), certifications (BLS/ACLS), a clinical skills section, 2–4 experience bullets with the units worked and patients handled, and education with your nursing degree and marks.

How do I write a nursing resume as a fresher?

Lead with your clinical rotations and internship: state the department, duration and skills practised. Add your registration, BLS certification, a clinical skills section and education. Our AI can turn each rotation into clear, ATS-friendly bullets.

Which skills are most important on a staff nurse resume?

Core clinical skills — patient care, medication administration, IV therapy and monitoring — plus the units you have worked (ICU/ER/OT) and certifications like BLS and ACLS.
