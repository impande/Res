What to include in a data analyst resume

  • Professional summary — 3–4 lines with your years of experience, top tools, and one quantified win.
  • Technical skills — List SQL, Excel and a language (Python/R) plus a BI tool — these are the exact keywords ATS screens for.
  • Quantified experience — Every bullet should have a number: rows processed, time saved, % improvement, revenue impact.
  • Projects (for freshers) — If you have little work experience, add 2–3 analysis projects with the dataset, tools and finding.
  • Education & certifications — Degree, plus any Google Data Analytics, Microsoft Power BI or SQL certifications.

Frequently asked questions

What should a data analyst resume include?

A short professional summary, a technical skills section (SQL, Excel, Python, a BI tool), 2–4 quantified experience bullets per role, projects if you are a fresher, and education with any relevant certifications.

How do I write a data analyst resume with no experience?

Lead with projects instead of jobs: pick 2–3 real datasets, state the tools you used and the insight you found, and quantify it. Add internships, a strong skills section and relevant certifications. Our AI can turn each project into ATS-friendly bullets.

Which skills are most important on a data analyst resume?

SQL and Excel are essential, plus at least one of Python or R and one BI tool (Power BI or Tableau). Add statistics, data cleaning and A/B testing if you have them.
