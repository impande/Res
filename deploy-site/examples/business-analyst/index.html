What to include in a business analyst resume

  • Professional summary — State your years, domains (banking, SaaS, retail) and one quantified business outcome.
  • Core BA skills — Requirements gathering, process mapping, SQL, Agile/Scrum, JIRA, UAT — the ATS keywords for BA roles.
  • Outcome-focused experience — Frame each bullet around impact: time saved, cost reduced, adoption or accuracy improved.
  • Tools & documentation — BRD/FRD, user stories, wireframes, dashboards — show the artefacts you actually produce.
  • Education & certifications — Degree plus CBAP, PMI-PBA, or a Scrum certification if you have one.

Frequently asked questions

What does a good business analyst resume look like?

A concise summary, a skills section with BA keywords (requirements, SQL, Agile, JIRA, UAT), and experience bullets framed as outcomes — time saved, cost reduced, adoption improved — plus your education and any BA certifications.

How do I make a business analyst resume with no experience?

Highlight transferable work: analysis coursework, a capstone or internship where you gathered requirements or mapped a process, and tools like Excel, SQL and JIRA. Quantify wherever you can and add a Scrum or CBAP-track certification.

Which certifications help a business analyst resume?

CBAP or ECBA (IIBA), PMI-PBA, and a Scrum certification (CSM/PSM) are the most recognised. List them in a dedicated certifications line.
