Ask for a Hike: A salary negotiation game for appraisal season

Ask for a Hike is a free salary negotiation game that turns appraisal season into a battle of nerves. Sit across 14 different bosses, from a startup founder to a government officer, and choose your moves: show your wins, quote market data, ask for perks or bluff an outside offer. Push too hard and you walk out with no salary hike at all. Every meeting ends with a real negotiation tip you can use at work.

  • 14 bosses with different personalities and tells to learn
  • Tactics: wins, market data, perks, silence and the outside-offer bluff
  • Career mode across many years with job switches
  • A Daily boss and friend challenges on the same meeting
  • Real negotiation tips after every meeting

How to play Ask for a Hike

  1. Each level is one appraisal meeting with a different boss. Pick your moves: show your wins, quote market data, ask for perks, stay silent or bluff an outside offer.
  2. Watch the boss's face and the stress gauge. Push too far and you walk out with no hike.
  3. Tap "Take the deal" to lock in your hike before patience runs out. Beat the target shown before the meeting for 3 stars.
  4. 14 bosses to crack, an endless Career mode with job switches, and a Daily boss. Your boss file fills in as you learn each boss's tells.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Ask for a Hike FAQ

Can a game really help with salary negotiation?

It helps you practise the basics: anchoring, reading the other side, knowing when to stop and asking for non-cash perks. Each meeting ends with a practical tip.

What is a good salary hike in India?

Typical annual hikes are often in the single digits, while job switches can bring much bigger jumps. A strong resume makes switching easier.

Is Ask for a Hike free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser on phone or laptop.
