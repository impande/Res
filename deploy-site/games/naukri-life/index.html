Naukri Life: A life simulator game about an Indian career

Naukri Life is a free life simulator game about growing up and getting a job in India. Pick Engineering, Commerce or Arts and live from age 21 to 40, one choice at a time: campus placements, your first appraisal, a job switch, family pressure for shaadi, an onsite chance, a startup idea or a government exam. Every choice changes your Pay, Joy, Skill and Family meters, and old decisions come back later.

  • Over 100 life events plus random surprises
  • 6 chapters played as 12 levels with star goals
  • 20 different endings to collect in the Endings Album
  • Achievements and unlockable looks for your character
  • A Daily Life that everyone plays with the same luck

How to play Naukri Life

  1. Pick Engineering, Commerce or Arts and live a whole Indian career from 21 to 40, one choice at a time.
  2. Every choice moves your Pay, Joy, Skill and Family meters. Risky moves show their odds, and old choices come back later to help or haunt you.
  3. Play the 6 chapters (College, First Job, Switch, Shaadi, 30s, 40) as 12 levels with star goals, or live a full new life. Stars come from your weakest meter at 40, so balance matters.
  4. Collect all 20 endings in the Endings Album, earn achievements and new looks, and try the Daily Life that everyone plays with the same luck today.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Naukri Life FAQ

Is Naukri Life like other life simulator games?

It is a short, choice-based life simulator focused on Indian careers and family life, from college to age 40.

How long does one life take?

About 5 minutes for a full life, so you can try different streams and endings.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser on phone or laptop.
