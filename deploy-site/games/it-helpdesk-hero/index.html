    IT Helpdesk Hero: A fast tapping game for the office IT hero

    IT Helpdesk Hero is a free tapping game where you are the only IT person in a growing office. Problems pop up over colleagues' desks and each one needs its own quick move: tap to clear a printer jam, swipe to plug in Wi-Fi, hold to reboot a blue screen, drag a spare mouse from the cart. Keep everyone happy from 9 AM to 6 PM as the company grows to a second floor and a server room.

    • 18 days with new problems: VPN outages, password resets, phishing
    • Different gestures for different problems: tap, swipe, hold, drag
    • Tool shop upgrades and an intern helper between days
    • Endless overtime and a Daily shift
    • Unlockable outfits and desk mascots

    How to play IT Helpdesk Hero

    1. Tap Start Day. Problems pop up as bubbles over colleagues' desks, each with a patience ring.
    2. Every problem has its own fix: swipe to plug in Wi-Fi, tap to clear a printer jam, hold to reboot a blue screen, drag a spare mouse or keyboard from the cart.
    3. 18 days as the company grows to a second floor and a server room, with VPN outages, password-reset queues, phishing and the boss's urgent printout. Keep colleagues happy for 3 stars.
    4. Spend your budget in the Tool shop between days, then try Endless overtime or the Daily shift. Stars unlock outfits and desk mascots.

    Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

    IT Helpdesk Hero FAQ

    Is IT Helpdesk Hero a tapping game?

    Yes. It is a fast tapping game, but each problem needs a different gesture, so it stays fresh across 18 days.

    Can I play on a laptop?

    Yes. It works with touch on phones and with the mouse and keyboard on laptops.

    Is it free?

    Yes. It is free to play in your browser with no download.
