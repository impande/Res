Who Stole My Tiffin?: A detective mystery game about the office lunch thief

Who Stole My Tiffin? is a free detective game for anyone who has lost a lunchbox at work. Every case is a short mystery: flip picture clue cards, watch suspects get cleared, accuse the thief and chase them down the rice trail. It is simple enough for a first-time player and gets trickier with two-part clues, lying witnesses and time clues.

  • 12 mystery cases in The Great Tiffin Heist season
  • Clear, picture-based clues that rule suspects out automatically
  • A chase finale, lineup spotlight and a confession for every case
  • A Daily case and friend challenges on the same case
  • Detective badges and desk themes to unlock

How to play Who Stole My Tiffin?

  1. Someone ate a colleague's lunch. Tap a face-down clue card to flip it.
  2. Anyone who doesn't match the clue is CLEARED automatically.
  3. When only one suspect is left (or you're sure), tap them, then Accuse! A wrong guess costs a heart.
  4. Chase the thief along the rice trail and grab them. Solve with fewer clues for 3 stars, and crack all 12 cases of The Great Tiffin Heist to unmask the Dabba Bandit.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Who Stole My Tiffin? FAQ

Is this detective game free?

Yes. Who Stole My Tiffin? is free to play in your browser on phone or laptop, with no download.

Is every mystery solvable?

Yes. Every case is checked to have exactly one possible thief, so careful detectives can always solve it.

How long is each case?

Most cases take 2 to 4 minutes, which makes it a good quick game for a lunch break.
