    How to play Chai Tapri Tycoon

    1. Pick a mode. Chai Yatra is an adventure across 12 stops against your rival Bunty Bhai; My Tapri is your own chai business that keeps earning while you're away.
    2. In Chai Yatra, tap the item each customer shows in their bubble before their patience runs out. Win stars, beat the boss stops (a chai-off, a food critic, the Chai King showdown) and decide what to do when a surprise blocks the road.
    3. For ⭐ customers and Masterbrew orders, tap the right ingredients, then hold to boil and let go in the green zone for a perfect cup.
    4. In My Tapri, tap to sell, hire staff like Chotu, add snacks and grow from cart to factory. Decorate your stall, unlock recipes, play the Daily shift and challenge friends on WhatsApp.
