    How to play Chai Tapri Tycoon

    1. Tap the Chai button to brew and sell. Spend your cash on upgrades, staff and snacks like samosa, bun maska and Maggi that earn money every second.
    2. Catch timed events (rain rush, cricket match crowd, IT park fest) for bonus sales. Your stalls keep earning while you're away.
    3. Grow from a cart to a café, a chain and a factory across 15 cities. Each city has 3 goals for 3 stars, and every star adds fame everywhere.
    4. Play Rush Hour: 3 minutes at the counter serving orders before customers walk out. It's also the Daily Rush and the mode your friends' challenges use. Stars unlock stall themes and cup styles.
