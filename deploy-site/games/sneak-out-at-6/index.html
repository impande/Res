Sneak Out at 6: An office stealth game about leaving on time

Sneak Out at 6 is a free stealth game about every office worker's daily mission: leaving on time. Hold to sneak towards the exit and let go the moment a colleague turns around. Hide behind plants and pillars, tiptoe over squeaky tiles, decline surprise meeting invites and outsmart HR on patrol, the CCTV camera and the "Chai?" intern.

  • 15 levels across 3 office weeks, Monday to Friday
  • New obstacles each day: HR patrols, CCTV, squeaky tiles and meeting invites
  • Hiding spots and a phone you can throw as a distraction
  • Endless floors mode and a Daily office layout
  • Unlockable outfits and bags

How to play Sneak Out at 6

  1. Hold "Hold to sneak" (or Space) to walk to the exit. Let go when a colleague turns: "?" is a warning, "!" means they can see you.
  2. Duck behind plants, pillars and the water cooler, and tiptoe over squeaky tiles.
  3. Every day adds new trouble: HR on patrol, CCTV, surprise meeting invites, the "Chai?" intern, and a phone you can throw as a distraction.
  4. 15 levels across 3 weeks, an Endless floors mode and a Daily office. Stars unlock outfits and bags.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Sneak Out at 6 FAQ

How do I play this stealth game?

Hold the Sneak button (or Space) to walk and let go when someone turns. A question mark is a warning; an exclamation mark means you have been seen.

Is Sneak Out at 6 free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser on phone or laptop, with no download.

How many levels are there?

15 career levels, plus an Endless mode and a new Daily office every day.
