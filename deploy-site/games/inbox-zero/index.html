Inbox Zero: An email game that teaches you to spot phishing scams

Inbox Zero is a free email game that doubles as a phishing quiz. Swipe each email right to reply, left for spam, up for urgent and down to archive before the timer runs out. Along the way you learn to spot real scams: fake job offers that ask for a fee, KYC update scams, gift-card requests from a fake "CEO" and lookalike company domains. Every mistake teaches you a one-line rule.

  • 18 workdays with a new scam type almost every day
  • Tap links and senders to see where they really go
  • Scam Spotter Handbook that fills as you learn
  • Power-ups: coffee, snooze and auto-filter
  • Overtime mode and a Daily inbox

How to play Inbox Zero

  1. Swipe each email: right to Reply, left for Spam, up for Urgent, down to Archive (or use the buttons and arrow keys). Each email has a timer.
  2. Tap a link or sender to see where it really goes. Fake job offers asking a fee, KYC scams, gift-card requests from the "CEO" and lookalike domains all go to Spam.
  3. 18 workdays add a new trick almost every day, plus Coffee, Snooze and Auto-filter power-ups. Reach Inbox Zero with perfect accuracy and speed for 3 stars.
  4. Every scam you miss teaches you how to spot it and goes into your Scam Spotter Handbook. Play Overtime or the Daily inbox, and unlock desk themes.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Inbox Zero FAQ

How do I spot a fake job offer?

Genuine employers never ask you to pay a fee for a job, training or an offer letter. Check the sender's domain and never share OTPs or bank details.

Is Inbox Zero a phishing quiz?

Yes. Many emails are realistic phishing and job scams, and each wrong answer shows how to spot that scam.

Is it free to play?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser with no download.
