Corporate Ladder: A 2048 game with an office twist

Corporate Ladder is a free 2048 game where the numbers are job titles. Swipe to slide every badge on the board, and two matching titles merge into the next rank: Intern, Trainee, Associate, Team Lead, Manager, Director, VP and finally CEO. Unlike the classic 2048 puzzle, each level has a goal, a move limit and office surprises like coffee, appraisal letters and pink slips.

  • 20 career levels across 4 office floors, each with a new twist
  • Drawn portraits for every rank, from intern lanyard to CEO gold tie
  • Endless mode and a Daily board that is the same for everyone
  • 5 unlockable office themes: startup, co-working, MNC, govt office, boardroom
  • Challenge friends on WhatsApp to beat your score on the same board

How to play Corporate Ladder

  1. Swipe (or use arrow keys) to slide every badge. Two matching titles merge into the next rank, from Intern all the way to CEO.
  2. Career mode has 20 levels across 4 office floors. Each level has a goal and a move limit: finish with moves to spare for 3 stars.
  3. New tiles appear as you climb: coffee and appraisal letters promote badges, pink slips and locked cabins get in your way, frozen badges need a merge next to them.
  4. Play Endless to chase CEO, or the Daily board, which is the same for everyone today. Stars unlock new office themes.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Corporate Ladder FAQ

Is Corporate Ladder like 2048?

Yes. It plays like the 2048 merge puzzle: swipe to slide tiles and merge matching ones. Instead of numbers you merge job titles, and each level adds goals and office power tiles.

Can I play Corporate Ladder on my phone?

Yes. Swipe on your phone or use the arrow keys on a laptop. It runs in the browser with no download.

What is the highest rank?

CEO. Reaching CEO in Endless mode is the ultimate goal, and the Daily board lets you compare scores with friends.
