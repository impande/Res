How to play Corporate Ladder

  1. Swipe (or use arrow keys) to slide every badge on the board.
  2. Two matching titles merge into the next one up: Intern → Trainee → Associate … → CEO.
  3. ☕ Coffee promotes a badge once, 🎁 Appraisal twice, 📉 Layoff clears a badge to make space.
  4. The game ends when no moves are left. Try to reach CEO 👑.
