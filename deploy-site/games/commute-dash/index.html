Commute Dash: A scooty and bike traffic game set in Indian cities

Commute Dash is a free scooty game and bike traffic racer set on real-feeling Indian roads. Ride through Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Gurugram traffic, change lanes to dodge autos, buses, cows, potholes and barricades, and grab cutting chai for a speed boost. Every level is a morning commute: punch in by 9:30 or explain yourself to the boss.

  • 16 career levels across 4 cities with rain, smog, signals and metro closures
  • 8 unlockable rides: scooty, cycle, e-scooter, bike and an office auto
  • Close-miss bonuses, chai power-ups and boss calls you must decline
  • Endless mode and a Daily route with a streak
  • Works with swipe on phones and arrow keys on laptops

How to play Commute Dash

  1. Swipe or tap left and right (or ← →) to change lanes. Swipe down (or ↓) to brake at signals.
  2. Dodge autos, buses, cows, potholes and barricades. Grab chai for a few seconds of invincibility, and ride close past traffic for a "Close one!" bonus.
  3. 16 career levels across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Gurugram add rain, wrong-side drivers, metro closures, smog and boss calls. Reach office by 9:30 with the chai target for 3 stars.
  4. Play Endless or the Daily route, and unlock 8 rides in the Garage: from the Desi Cycle to the Thumper Bike and an Office Auto.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Commute Dash FAQ

Is Commute Dash a free bike game?

Yes. It is a free browser bike and scooty game with no download, no sign-up and no ads.

How do I control the scooty?

Swipe or tap left and right to change lanes and swipe down to brake. On a laptop use the arrow keys.

Which cities are in the game?

Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Gurugram, each with its own skyline, traffic and weather.
