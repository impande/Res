Office Cricket: An online cricket batting game in the office corridor

Office Cricket is a free cricket game you can play in your browser. Bat from behind the stumps in an office corridor while colleagues bowl paper balls at you. Time your tap for fours and sixes, swing early to hit leg side and late to hit off side, and find the gaps between fielding colleagues. Smash the water cooler for bonus runs, but break the meeting-room glass and you lose five.

  • The Corridor Cup: 16 matches against departments, ending with the Boss
  • Bouncers, yorkers, swing and spin to read and time
  • TV-style scoreboard, commentary and slow-motion sixes
  • Endless Super Over and a Daily match
  • Unlockable bats, jerseys and helmets, including a keyboard bat

How to play Office Cricket

  1. Tap the pitch, the HIT button or press Space as the ball reaches the gold ring. Swing early to hit left, late to hit right, and find the gaps between fielding colleagues.
  2. Perfect timing is a SIX, good timing a FOUR. Smash the water cooler for bonus runs, but break the meeting-room glass and you lose 5.
  3. The Corridor Cup is 16 matches against HR XI, Sales Strikers, IT Titans and more, with bouncers, swing, yorkers and spin, ending in the Appraisal Cup Final against the Boss. Win with balls to spare for 3 stars.
  4. Play the endless Super Over or the Daily match, and unlock bats, jerseys and helmets (including a keyboard bat).

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Office Cricket FAQ

Is Office Cricket a free online cricket game?

Yes. It is a free cricket batting game that runs in your browser on phone or laptop, with no download.

How do I hit a six?

Tap the pitch, the HIT button or press Space exactly when the ball reaches the gold ring. Perfect timing gives a six.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. After a match, challenge friends on WhatsApp to chase your score against the same deliveries.
