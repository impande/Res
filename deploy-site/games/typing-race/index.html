Typing Race: A typing speed test game with an SSC-style typing test

Typing Race is a free typing speed test turned into a race. Type the passage to move your racer on an office chair, and watch your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy live. Practise for government jobs with the SSC-style typing test mode, which uses a 35 WPM pass mark and up to 5% wrong words, then download a typing certificate with your name and score.

  • 1, 2 and 5-minute typing tests with WPM and accuracy
  • Government-style typing test with a 35 WPM pass mark
  • Career league of 16 races against faster colleagues
  • Works with phone keyboards, including autocorrect
  • Downloadable typing certificate (PNG)

How to play Typing Race

  1. Tap Continue to jump into your next race. Your progress is saved after every race, and you can replay any race from All races.
  2. Type the passage to roll your chair forward. Backspace fixes mistakes, and clean word streaks fire nitro.
  3. Finish in the top 3 to unlock the next race. After race 16 the league never ends: new colleagues, numbers, Hinglish, boss visits, sudden death and deadlines, with a season finale every 10th race.
  4. Also try the 1, 2 and 5-minute tests, the govt-style test (35 WPM pass mark), Time Attack and the Daily Passage, and download your typing certificate.

Free to play, no download and no sign-up. Works on phones and laptops. When you finish, challenge your friends on WhatsApp.

Typing Race FAQ

What typing speed is needed for SSC?

For many SSC and government data-entry posts, the English typing requirement is around 35 words per minute. Check the official notification for your exam.

Can I get a typing certificate?

Yes. After a test you can download a typing certificate image with your name, WPM and accuracy. It is a practice certificate, not an official one.

Can I take this typing test on my phone?

Yes. The test works with phone keyboards as well as laptop keyboards.

Career tip

Many data-entry and government typing tests ask for about 35 WPM in English. Fast typist? Make a data-entry resume.
