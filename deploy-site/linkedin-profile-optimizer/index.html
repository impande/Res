What makes a LinkedIn profile show up in recruiter search

Recruiters search LinkedIn by job title and skills. Your headline, About section, skills and experience are what that search reads. This tool writes each of them from your resume:

  • Headline (220 characters) — role, specialism and a proof point, in three styles to choose from.
  • About — a hook in the first two lines, your real achievements with numbers, and the keywords recruiters type.
  • Top skills — 10 skills in LinkedIn's own naming, so they match recruiter filters.
  • Experience — short, action-led descriptions for each role.

LinkedIn optimizer FAQ

How long should a LinkedIn headline be?

Up to 220 characters. Put your role and specialism first — that's what shows in search results.

How long should the About section be?

1,300–2,000 characters works best (2,600 max). The first two lines show before "see more", so they need to hook.

Will it invent achievements?

No. It only uses facts from your resume and leaves [X] where you should add a real number.
