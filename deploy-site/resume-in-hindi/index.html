When a resume in Hindi or your regional language helps

Government departments, state PSUs, schools, cooperative banks and many local employers welcome — and sometimes prefer — resumes in Hindi or the state language. A well-written regional resume also helps for roles where you'll work with local customers.

This tool doesn't translate word-for-word. It rewrites your resume in the professional register employers in that language actually use, keeps company names and technical skills in English, and never adds or removes facts.
