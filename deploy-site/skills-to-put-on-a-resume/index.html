Short answer: list 8–12 relevant skills that mix hard skills (tools, software, languages) and soft skills (communication, teamwork), matched to the job description — in a dedicated Skills section and inside your experience bullets.

Hard skills vs soft skills

Hard skills are specific, teachable and measurable — a programming language, a design tool, a certification. Soft skills are how you work — communication, adaptability, leadership. Recruiters want both: hard skills prove you can do the job, soft skills prove you'll do it well with others.

How to choose the right skills

  • Mirror the job description — list the skills it names, as long as you genuinely have them.
  • Prioritise hard, role-specific skills — these are what ATS and recruiters scan for first.
  • Back the important ones up — show at least one in action in your experience bullets.
  • Drop the obvious and the fake — "MS Word" and skills you can't demonstrate add nothing.

Where to put skills on your resume

Use two places: a clean Skills section (a scannable list ATS reads easily) and your experience bullets, where you prove a skill with a result — e.g. "Used SQL and Power BI to build a sales dashboard that cut reporting time by 30%." Listing a skill is good; showing it in action is better.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best skills to put on a resume?

A mix of hard skills (tools, software, languages specific to the job) and soft skills (communication, teamwork, problem-solving). The best skills are the ones that appear in the job description and that you can actually back up with examples.

What is the difference between hard and soft skills?

Hard skills are teachable, measurable abilities like Python, Excel, SEO or AutoCAD. Soft skills are how you work with others — communication, leadership, time management. A strong resume shows both.

How many skills should I list on a resume?

Around 8–12 relevant skills in your skills section, then weave a few more into your experience bullet points. Quality and relevance beat a long, generic list.

Where should I put skills on a resume?

In a dedicated 'Skills' section (easy for ATS and recruiters to scan) and inside your experience bullets, where you show the skill in action with a result.

How do I know which skills to include?

Read the job description and mirror the skills it asks for (if you genuinely have them). Our AI builder can suggest role-relevant skills automatically.
