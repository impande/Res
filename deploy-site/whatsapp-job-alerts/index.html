Why job alerts on WhatsApp beat job portals

Good openings fill fast, and early applicants are often the first to be seen. Checking several portals every day takes time; a short WhatsApp message each morning brings the new openings to you.

Frequently asked questions

Are these WhatsApp job alerts free?

Yes. Job alerts are completely free. You only send one WhatsApp message to subscribe, and you can stop anytime by replying "stop alerts".

How do I get daily job alerts on WhatsApp?

Enter the role you want (and a city, if you like) above and tap "Get alerts on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with your request typed — tap Send. You'll get today's matching jobs straight away and a fresh list every morning.

Which jobs will I get?

Fresh openings for your role and city from Indian job listings, ranked by how well they match your role — and your skills, if you've built your resume with us. You also get one-tap links to the last 24 hours of jobs on Naukri, LinkedIn and Indeed.

How many messages will I get?

One message a day, in the morning, and only when there are new jobs you haven't been sent before. No groups, no forwards, no spam.

How do I change my role or city, or stop alerts?

Send "job alerts <role> in <city>" to change it — for example "job alerts data analyst in Bangalore". Reply "stop alerts" to stop. You can switch them back on any time.

Do I need a resume?

No. Alerts work with just a role. If you build your resume on Resume For You, alerts are also matched to your skills, and you can apply with a polished, ATS-friendly resume.

Is my number shared with anyone?

No. Your number is used only to send you the alerts you asked for. It is not added to any group, and other users can't see it.
